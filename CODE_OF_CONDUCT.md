# Code of Conduct

DavNode follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good intent, accept criticism of your work
gracefully, and do not harass or discriminate against anyone. This applies
to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through
[GitHub Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/kaigothe/DavNode/security/advisories/new)
(choose "other" as the topic) or by contacting the maintainer, Kai Gothe,
through the e-mail address on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and
restrict participation to uphold this code, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Covenant.
